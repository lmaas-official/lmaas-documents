# Security and Data Handling

Last checked: 2026-11-12 (Japanese original) / Sources: https://lmaas.io/#trust , https://lmaas.io/terms , https://lmaas.io/privacy

This document is a summary. The formal terms are governed by the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.

## What lmaas.io does

### Your data is not sent to external AI services

- Documents, training data and conversations are processed inside the lmaas.io environment and are not sent to external AI services such as ChatGPT.
- AI computation runs on the facilities of cloud GPU providers contracted by us.
- **Exception:** if a customer sets up an external connection (MCP or API), data is sent to that destination. How the data is handled there depends on the terms of the destination service.

### Environments are separated per customer

- AI environments are separated for each customer; the basic design is that they are not mixed with other customers' environments.
- On shared-GPU plans, the GPU (computer) is shared by multiple customers and requests are processed in order.

### Stored data is encrypted

- Customers' training data and trained data are stored encrypted.

### Administrators manage usage

- Only administrators can change settings. Users can only use the AI and connections assigned to them.
- Only administrators register connections to external services, and administrators decide which users can use them.
- On Business and Business Plus, administrators can review all users' conversations (including those via API). Even if a user deletes a conversation, it only disappears from that user's screen; administrators can still review it for 180 days after deletion. Personal, Starter and Starter Plus do not have this feature.

### AI model licenses are checked

- We check licenses and related matters before listing an AI model.
- Each model shows whether "commercial use", "additional training" and "redistribution" are allowed, and consent to a license is recorded together with who consented and when.

### Making it clear that content was created by AI

- The chat screen always shows that you are talking with an AI.
- The AI is instructed in advance to answer that it is an AI when asked whether it is a human.
- When used from external systems via API, replies carry a marker showing that they were generated by AI.
- Each time the AI creates an answer, a signed generation record is created. The record does not contain the text of the answer itself; it stores a fingerprint (hash) for matching. Records are kept for 180 days after creation and then deleted automatically.
- By sending a text to the verification service, you can check whether it is an answer created by lmaas.io's AI. If the text has been altered it may not be found, and not finding it does not mean the text was not created by AI.
- These are features that address the AI disclosure and identification of AI-generated content required by Article 50 of the EU AI Act. They do not mean that the service or a customer's use complies with EU law.

## What lmaas.io does not guarantee

Because it is needed to make an informed decision, we also state clearly what we do not guarantee.

- **The accuracy of AI answers.** AI does not always generate accurate answers. lmaas.io provides an environment for using AI and does not guarantee the accuracy of answers or their fitness for a particular purpose.
- **The safety of AI models and trained AI.** We cannot guarantee that they will not output harmful or incorrect content or behave in unintended ways. Before using them in your work, please check the output and its safety yourself.
- **Answer capability when using MCP or AI tools.** It varies by model, and the AI may give incorrect answers.
- **Response speed.** It changes with usage and external factors, so it is not guaranteed on any plan.
- **Safety at external connection destinations.** External connections (MCP and API) set up by the customer are outside the scope of lmaas.io's security.

## Discontinuation of models

- Some models may become unavailable due to license changes or discontinuation by the model provider, safety problems, and so on.
- The licenses held by us and the licenses the customer agrees to are managed separately.
