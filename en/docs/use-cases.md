# Use Cases

Last checked: 2026-11-12 (Japanese original)

This page describes concrete situations where lmaas.io helps. The "Suggested plan" for each case is only a reference. Choose based on the model size you need, the number of users and the response speed you need ([Pricing](pricing.md)).

> The examples here are hypothetical, written to explain how the features are used. They are not case studies of specific customers.

---

## 1. Answer questions about internal rules and manuals based on your own documents

**Common problem**
General affairs, HR and IT departments receive the same questions again and again: "What does this rule say?" "What is the application procedure?" You want to avoid entering internal rules into external AI.

**How to use lmaas.io**
1. Register PDFs such as work rules, expense rules and internal manuals (RAG)
2. Users ask questions in AI chat
3. The AI searches and refers to the registered documents to answer

**Points**
- Documents are not sent to external AI services
- When rules are revised, you can update only the reference data (RAG). You can also go back to the previous version

**Suggested plan:** Starter or higher (Starter Plus or Business if there are many users)

---

## 2. Summarize inquiries and register them in an internal management system

**Common problem**
Messages received through the contact form are long, and staff spend time reading, categorizing and summarizing them before entering them into the management system. They contain customer information, so you do not want to send them to external AI services.

**How to use lmaas.io**
1. Your system calls lmaas.io's API and passes the inquiry text
2. The AI returns a draft summary and category
3. Your system registers the result in the management system

**Points**
- Administrators issue and revoke API keys
- Replies carry a marker showing that they were generated by AI

**Suggested plan:** If there are many inquiries or you need fast responses, a dedicated GPU (Starter Plus, Business Plus)

Detailed flow: [examples/inquiry-workflow.md](../examples/inquiry-workflow.md)

## 3. Grow an AI that has learned your terminology and past cases

**Common problem**
General-purpose AI does not know industry terminology or your company's way of phrasing things, so its answers cannot be used as they are.

**How to use lmaas.io**
1. Prepare past example answers, Q&A and explanations of internal terms as text
2. Choose the training strength (Low, Standard, High) and reserve additional training (LoRA)
3. When training finishes, you can use an AI that has learned how your company answers

**Points**
- Training results are saved by version; if the results are not what you wanted, you can go back to a previous version or to the base model
- Combined with internal document reference (RAG), you get closer to an AI that "knows your internal information and answers in your company's style"

**Suggested plan:** If you train often, Starter Plus or Business Plus, which offer training-only mode

## 4. Gather internal information and draft reports (MCP integration)

**Common problem**
For weekly and monthly reports, staff gather information from calendars, inquiry management tools and internal documents and compile it by hand.

**How to use lmaas.io**
1. An administrator registers the external services and internal systems (MCP) the AI may connect to, and decides which users can use them
2. A user asks in AI chat, "Summarize this week's status"
3. The AI retrieves the information it needs from the connections and drafts a report

**Points**
- Connections to external services require administrator permission
- When connected to external services, data is sent to the destination. The safety of the destination is outside the scope of lmaas.io
- Answer capability with MCP varies by model, and the AI may give incorrect answers

**Suggested plan:** Business or Business Plus, which can use 24B-class or larger models, is recommended (small models are sometimes poor at using tools)

## 5. Manage employees' use of generative AI as a company

**Common problem**
Employees use various AI services at their own discretion, and you do not know what is being entered.

**How to use lmaas.io**
1. Sign up for lmaas.io as a company; an administrator registers the users
2. Assign the AI and connections each user may use
3. On Business and Business Plus, administrators can review users' conversations (for preventing misuse and audits)

**Suggested plan:** Depending on the number of users: Starter (up to 3), Starter Plus / Business (up to 10), Business Plus

---

## 6. Professionals and small businesses using AI for work with confidential documents

**Common problem**
In work that handles documents you do not want to put into external AI — clients' contracts, consultation records, design documents — you want to use AI to summarize documents and draft text.

**How to use lmaas.io**
1. Start with a smaller model on Personal
2. Narrow down models by industry (healthcare, insurance, etc.) or task (document summarization, etc.)
3. Let RAG refer to your documents as needed

**Points**
- The accuracy of AI answers is not guaranteed. Always check the output before using it in your work

**Suggested plan:** Personal (1 user)

---

## When lmaas.io is not a good fit

- When you need the same performance as the latest and largest general-purpose AI
- When you always need replies within a few seconds on a shared GPU (consider a dedicated-GPU plan)
- When you need models larger than the 32B class (please contact us individually)
