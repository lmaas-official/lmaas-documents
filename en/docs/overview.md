# lmaas.io Overview

Last checked: 2026-11-12 (Japanese original) / Source: https://lmaas.io (top page)

## What is lmaas.io?

lmaas.io is a cloud service that provides, all in one place, an environment for using AI (LLMs) so that customers can use generative AI in their work with confidence. Using AI models, connecting them to internal data, customizing the AI (additional training) and managing users all take place inside an environment separated for each customer.

Typical generative AI services are centered on "using AI". lmaas.io provides **your company × AI models × internal data × users** as an environment dedicated to you.

## The problems behind it

Customers who want to use generative AI at work often say:

- We are not sure whether we may enter internal documents into generative AI
- We are worried about how customer and confidential information is handled
- Employees are using AI services on their own
- Managing AI is hard with only our in-house IT staff
- We want AI to answer questions about our internal rules and manuals
- We want an AI of our own

## How lmaas.io approaches this

### 1. Your data is not sent to external AI services

Documents, training data and conversations are processed inside the lmaas.io environment and are not sent to external AI services such as ChatGPT. However, if a customer sets up an external connection (MCP or API), data is sent to that destination.

### 2. Not just "choosing" an AI, but "growing" it

General-purpose AIs such as ChatGPT and Gemini are excellent, but they do not know your internal rules, procedures, product knowledge, past cases or terminology from the start. lmaas.io aims for an "AI that understands your business" by combining:

- The power of publicly available AI models
- Search and reference of internal documents (RAG)
- Additional training on your own data (LoRA)
- Re-choosing the AI model as needed

### 3. Expand step by step

1. Start by using it as an AI chat
2. Register internal documents and let RAG refer to them
3. Train it on your business data and grow it for your company
4. Connect it to business systems with API and MCP

## What lmaas.io does not aim to be

lmaas.io is not a service that competes on performance with ChatGPT or Gemini themselves. Performance on general questions and writing depends on the AI model you choose.
