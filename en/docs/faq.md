# Frequently Asked Questions

Last checked: 2026-11-18 (Japanese original) / Source: https://lmaas.io/#faq ("Service basics" to "AI generation records" follow the official site. "Applying and contracts" summarizes the pricing table and the contact page)

## Service basics

### What is lmaas.io?

It is a service that provides each customer with its own environment for running AI, so you can grow the AI with your own data and use it in your business. Choose from over 1,000 public AI models and combine them with additional training and references to your internal documents (RAG).

### Is it a service like ChatGPT?

lmaas.io has an AI chat feature, but it is not just a chat service. It is an AI environment for your company that manages your AI environment, users, internal data and AI models. lmaas.io runs publicly available AI models in an environment dedicated to you, and you grow them with your own data. We do not send your data to external AI services.

### Is my data sent outside?

It is not sent to external AI services. If you set up an external connection (MCP or API), data is sent to that destination. AI computation runs on the facilities of cloud GPU providers contracted by us.

## Internal data and training

### Can the AI read our internal documents and answer from them?

Yes. Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF documents can be turned into training data with "Training Data Creation", and after checking the contents you can use them for training or RAG (they are not sent to external AI services). PDF documents can also be used directly as a dedicated reference for the AI with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and used in its answers.

### How does the AI training work?

Only a small "add-on" (LoRA) is trained, without changing the AI model itself. The AI learns to follow the way your registered Q&A and texts answer and are written. Each document is read up to about 2,000 tokens, and anything longer is cut off.

### How many times can the AI be trained?

Each plan has a set number of training runs per 30 days (Personal 5, Starter 5, Starter Plus 5, Business 8, Business Plus 10). The 30-day periods are counted from the start of your use (the day your environment was created). They are separate from billing periods; annual plans also use 30-day periods. If you need more, you can add runs at $5 (excluding tax; ¥800 for customers in Japan) each. With the dedicated-GPU plans (Starter Plus, Business Plus), you can also switch the server you use for chat to training-only mode and train without a limit on the number of runs. While training-only mode is on, the server cannot be used for chat or other inference.

### What happens to my training runs if I change plans mid-period?

From the period in which you change, the new plan's number of runs applies. If you upgrade, you can use the added runs right away. If you downgrade, what remains is the new plan's number minus the runs already used in that period. For example, if you use 3 runs on Business (8 runs) and then downgrade to Starter (5 runs), 2 runs remain. Purchased additional runs are not reduced in either case.

### If I cancel a training reservation, is the run returned?

Yes. It is returned to the period it was used in and to the source it was taken from (the plan's runs or purchased additional runs). Because it is returned as a run of a period that has already ended, if you cancel after the next period has started, the runs of the new period do not increase.

## Management and security

### Can administrators review employees' chats?

On Business and Business Plus, administrators can review all employees' chat conversations (including conversations via API). This feature is for preventing misuse and for audits. Even if an employee deletes a conversation, it only disappears from the employee's screen; administrators can still review it for 180 days after deletion. Personal, Starter and Starter Plus do not have this feature.

### Is the safety of AI models and trained AI guaranteed?

No. We check licenses and related matters before listing an AI model, but we cannot guarantee that AI models or trained AI will not output harmful or incorrect content, or that they will not behave in unintended ways. Before using them in your work, please check the output and its safety yourself.

### Are the AI's answers guaranteed?

AI does not always generate accurate answers. lmaas.io provides an environment for using AI and does not guarantee the accuracy of the answers the AI generates or their fitness for a particular purpose.

## AI generation records

### Is it clear when an answer was created by AI?

Yes. The chat screen always shows that you are talking with an AI. The AI is also instructed in advance to answer that it is an AI when asked whether it is a human. When used from external systems via API, replies also carry a marker showing that they were generated by AI. These are features that address the AI disclosure and identification of AI-generated content required by Article 50 of the EU AI Act.

### Are records kept of answers created by AI?

Yes. Each time the AI creates an answer, a signed record showing that it was generated by AI is created. You can download it for each answer on the chat screen, and when used from external systems, a record number is attached to the answer. The record does not contain the text of the answer itself; it stores a fingerprint for matching the text. Records are kept for 180 days after creation and then deleted automatically. When you cancel your contract, they are deleted in the same way as your other data. By sending a text to our verification service, you can check whether it is an answer made by lmaas.io's AI (only while the record is kept). If the text has been altered, it may not be found, and not finding it does not mean the text was not made by AI.

## Applying and contracts

### How much does it cost?

From $400/month for customers outside Japan and ¥64,000/month for customers in Japan (both for Personal). Prices exclude tax, and an initial fee applies separately. Annual payment is 10% off. See [Pricing](pricing.md) for details.

### Is there a minimum term?

Yes. 90 days from the start of the contract.

### How soon can we start?

After the service is activated, you can start using your dedicated AI in as little as 10 minutes (a rough guide).

### What payment methods are available?

At the time of application, you can choose credit card payment or bank transfer. Bank transfer is available only for customers in Japan (payment in Japanese yen).

### Can we apply from outside Japan?

Customers outside Japan can apply as businesses only. Fees are billed in US dollars. Applications are not accepted from some countries and regions.

### We want to use models larger than the 32B class.

The listed plans go up to the 32B class. For larger models such as the 70B class, or other individual requests, please contact us through the contact form.
